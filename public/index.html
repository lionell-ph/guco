About us

Support that's easy to reach

The Guidance and Counseling Office (GUCO) at BPSU Main Campus supports students through academic, personal, and emotional concerns, and coordinates with peer facilitators to make that support easier to reach.

Our approach

Meeting students where they are

Whether it's a scheduled session, a walk-in, or a first conversation with a peer facilitator, the goal is the same: make support easy to reach and confidential by default.

Who we serve

Main Campus students

Every student enrolled at BPSU Main Campus can request sessions, reach peer facilitators, and manage documents through this system.

How it works

One system, three roles

Students submit requests, GUCO personnel schedule and confirm them, and admins keep the records, evaluations, and reports straight.

Our commitment

Confidential, accountable care

Sessions stay private. Evidence, evaluations, and reports keep the office accountable to students and to the university.
