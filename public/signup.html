Create your account

Sign up with your BPSU email to request sessions and track your requests online.

Your name and student ID should match your school ID. Once your account is approved, they are filled in on every request for you.

Verify you're a BPSU student

Attach clear photos of the front and back of your school ID. Make sure your name and photo can be read. Only GUCO staff who review accounts can see it, and it is deleted once your account is reviewed.

  • All four corners of the ID are visible
  • Your name and photo are clear, with no glare or blur
  • Use your current BPSU student ID
How GUCO uses your ID

Your ID photos are used for one purpose: to check that the name and photo match the details you entered. Only GUCO staff who review accounts can see them. The photos are deleted as soon as your account is approved or rejected. Your other details are used to run your GUCO account and requests.
