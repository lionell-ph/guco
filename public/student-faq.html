Before you ask at the office

Common questions.

Answers to what students usually want to know before booking a session or submitting a request.

Is what I say confidential?

Yes. What you share with a counselor stays between you and the office, with the standard exception of situations where someone's safety is at immediate risk — the same limit any licensed counselor works under.

How soon can I get a session?

Most session requests are scheduled within 2–3 working days of submitting a request. If you're in crisis, don't wait for a slot — see the hotline at the top of this page, or come to the office directly.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome and are seen as counselors become available. Submitting a request ahead of time just means a counselor can prepare and you're less likely to wait.

What's the difference between a counselor and a peer facilitator?

Counselors are licensed professionals for ongoing or sensitive concerns. Peer facilitators are trained students who offer a first, informal conversation — a bridge to a counselor, not a replacement for one.

Will this affect my academic record?

Counseling sessions themselves don't appear on your academic record. Document requests like exit interviews or good moral certificates are processed separately and only shared as required for that specific purpose.
