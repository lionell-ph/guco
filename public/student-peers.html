What peer support is for

A first, informal conversation with a trained student — for everyday stress, adjustment, or just needing to talk. For anything ongoing or sensitive, a peer facilitator will point you to a counselor.

Are peer facilitators trained?

Yes. They complete training run by the office before taking duty hours, and they're supervised by the counseling staff.

Is talking to a peer facilitator confidential?

Peer facilitators keep conversations private, with the same safety exception counselors follow — if someone's safety is at immediate risk, that gets escalated to the office.
